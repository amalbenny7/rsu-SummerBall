Never been before?

Summer Ball is the biggest night in the student calendar. It’s the festival on your doorstep, open exclusively to Reading students and your guests.

Expect multiple stages, huge headliners, free fairground rides, performers, bars, food trucks and thousands of students celebrating the end of the year together. Make memories with your friends from sunset to sunrise and be there for the iconic survivor’s photo at 6am!

The line-up will be announced soon but don’t worry it will not disappoint. Previous years have included Arrdee, Vikkstar, RAYE, Caity Baser, Becky Hill, D.O.D, Sub Focus, Disciples, Scouting for Girls, Katy B and many more.

Last year sold out, so we recommend getting your ticket asap to avoid disappointment.

Black Friday FAQs

When is Summer Ball 2027?
Saturday 29th May.
Can I only buy a Black Friday ticket in person?
Yes, this is a Black Friday in-person sale. Come along to the stall outside the SU building on Thursday between 11.30am–3pm to buy your ticket.
Do I need to bring anything?
Please bring your student ID card and a payment card. We will not accept cash.
How will I get my Black Friday ticket?
The ticket will appear in your SU app in the “Tickets” section. Please note, the ticket might not come through immediately after payment.
Can I buy a Black Friday ticket for my friend?
You can buy a maximum of 2 tickets. Your friend must also be a student and you will need their student ID number. The ticket will be sent to their SU app.
Can I buy a Black Friday Guest ticket?
No, Black Friday tickets are for students only. You must have your student ID.
How much do I save?
This Black Friday sale saves you up to £27.50.
Can I get a refund?
No. Tickets for this event are non-refundable so please make sure you are able to attend before you buy your ticket.
