Never been before?

Summer Ball is the biggest night in the student calendar. It’s the festival on your doorstep, open exclusively to Reading students and your guests.

Expect multiple stages, huge headliners, free fairground rides, performers, bars, food trucks and thousands of students celebrating the end of the year together. Make memories with your friends from sunset to sunrise and be there for the iconic survivor’s photo at 6am!

The line-up will be announced soon but don’t worry it will not disappoint. Previous years have included Arrdee, Vikkstar, RAYE, Caity Baser, Becky Hill, D.O.D, Sub Focus, Disciples, Scouting for Girls, Katy B and many more.

Last year sold out, so we recommend getting your ticket asap to avoid disappointment.
